ABOUT THE ORGANIZATION

ARMED FORCES OF THE PHILIPPINES

The Armed Forces of the Philippines is the country's military organization composed of three major services.

UNDERSTANDING THE AFP

The Armed Forces of the Philippines, commonly known as the AFP, is organized into three major services responsible for land, maritime, and air military capabilities.

These services operate within the larger AFP organization while maintaining their own personnel, units, equipment, traditions, and specialized capabilities.

Military rank establishes a formal hierarchy and identifies an individual's grade and position within the organization.

ABOUT THIS WEBSITE

UNDERSTANDING AFP RANKS

This website provides an educational overview of the Armed Forces of the Philippines, its three major services, and the hierarchy of commissioned officer grades.

Rank titles differ between the Army, Air Force, and Navy even when the grades are equivalent. For example, Colonel in the Army and Air Force corresponds to Captain in the Philippine Navy.

Rank equivalencies are presented using the commissioned officer grades established under Philippine law.
