ABOUT THE ORGANIZATION

ARMED FORCES OF THE PHILIPPINES

The Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP) is the country's military organization. Its structure includes three major services, each with distinct operational roles and responsibilities.

MILITARY ORGANIZATION

The AFP is organized to provide military capabilities across land, maritime, and air domains. These capabilities are carried out through its major services and supporting commands and units.

Understanding military rank provides a way to identify an individual's position within the organizational hierarchy and the corresponding level of authority and responsibility.

ABOUT THIS WEBSITE

A SIMPLE GUIDE TO AFP RANKS

This website is designed as an educational reference for understanding the organization of the Armed Forces of the Philippines, its three major services, and the hierarchy of commissioned officer grades.

Rank titles may differ between the Army, Air Force, and Navy even when they correspond to the same officer grade. For example, Colonel in the Army and Air Force corresponds to Captain in the Navy.

Rank equivalencies are presented using the statutory commissioned-officer grades established under Philippine law.
