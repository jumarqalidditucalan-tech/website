ABOUT THE ORGANIZATION

ARMED FORCES OF THE PHILIPPINES

The Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP) is the country's military organization composed of three major services: the Philippine Army, Philippine Navy, and Philippine Air Force.

UNDERSTANDING THE AFP

The AFP is organized to provide military capabilities across land, maritime, and air domains. Each major service has its own organization, personnel, equipment, and specialized capabilities.

Military ranks establish a hierarchy that identifies an individual's grade, position, and level of authority within the organization.

ABOUT THIS WEBSITE

A GUIDE TO AFP RANKS

This website presents an educational overview of the Armed Forces of the Philippines, its three major services, and the hierarchy of commissioned officer grades.

Army and Air Force officers use titles such as General, Colonel, Major, and Captain, while the Philippine Navy uses corresponding naval titles such as Admiral, Captain, Lieutenant Commander, and Lieutenant.

Rank equivalencies are based on the commissioned officer grades established in Republic Act No. 11709.
