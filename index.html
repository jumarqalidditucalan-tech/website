ABOUT THE ORGANIZATION

ARMED FORCES OF THE PHILIPPINES

The Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP) is the country's military organization composed of three major services.

UNDERSTANDING THE AFP

The Armed Forces of the Philippines is organized into major services that provide military capabilities across land, maritime, and air domains.

Each service has its own organization, personnel, equipment, traditions, and specialized military functions while operating as part of the AFP.

Military ranks establish a formal hierarchy within the organization and identify an individual's grade and level of authority.

ABOUT THIS WEBSITE

UNDERSTANDING AFP RANKS

This website presents an educational overview of the Armed Forces of the Philippines, its three major services, and the commissioned officer hierarchy.

Although equivalent grades exist across the services, the titles used by the Philippine Navy differ from the Army and Air Force titles.

For example, Colonel corresponds to Captain in the Philippine Navy, while Major corresponds to Lieutenant Commander.

Rank equivalencies are based on the statutory commissioned officer grades established under Philippine law.
