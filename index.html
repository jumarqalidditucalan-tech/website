ABOUT THE ORGANIZATION

ARMED FORCES OF THE PHILIPPINES

The Armed Forces of the Philippines, commonly known as the AFP, is the country's military organization composed of three major services.

UNDERSTANDING THE AFP

The AFP is organized across land, maritime, and air domains through its three major services: the Philippine Army, Philippine Navy, and Philippine Air Force.

Each service has its own personnel, units, equipment, traditions, and specialized military capabilities while remaining part of the larger AFP organization.

Military rank provides a formal hierarchy that identifies an officer's grade and position within the organization.

ABOUT THIS WEBSITE

UNDERSTANDING AFP RANKS

This website provides an educational overview of the Armed Forces of the Philippines, its three major services, and the commissioned officer hierarchy.

Rank titles differ between the Army, Air Force, and Navy even when the corresponding officer grades are equivalent.

For example, Colonel in the Army and Air Force corresponds to Captain in the Philippine Navy.

Rank equivalencies are presented using the commissioned officer grades established under Philippine law.
